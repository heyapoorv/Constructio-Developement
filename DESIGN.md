## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the calculated precision, tectonic permanence, and structural elegance of modern luxury architecture. It addresses discerning private clients, institutional patrons, and design connoisseurs who value spatial purity, material honesty, and bespoke craftsmanship.

The visual style synthesizes **Architectural Minimalism** with **Editorial Precision**:
- **Monolithic Compositions:** Layouts rely on expansive negative space, structural rectilinear alignments, and disciplined proportional rhythm akin to floor plans and elevations.
- **Tectonic Depth:** Tactile contrasts between smooth, warm mineral surfaces and dark, dense monolithic masses.
- **Atmospheric Nuance:** Subdued glassmorphic details evoke polished structural glass and water features, while hairline dividers simulate technical drafting drawings.
- **Cinematic Framing:** Full-bleed, atmospheric architectural photography serves as structural foundation rather than decorative overlay.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw, noble architectural materials: travertine, cast bronze, honed limestone, raw aggregate concrete, and blackened steel.

- **Primary (`#121316`):** Deep Obsidian. Imparts gravity, grounding structural frames, primary typography, and authoritative accents. Paired with an elevated secondary dark tone (`#1A1C20`) for layered surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`):** Architectural Bronze. An understated, desaturated metallic accent applied sparingly for technical callouts, active indicators, and high-tier markers. Supported by brilliant accent gold (`#D4AF37`) strictly for fine focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#8E9096`):** Honed Concrete. Serves as a neutral structural bridge, handling hairline rules, technical coordinates, metadata, and secondary labels. A lighter tinted concrete (`#E5E5E2`) provides subtle field separation.
- **Neutral (`#FBFBF9`):** Warm Ivory. The primary canvas substrate, avoiding sterile digital white in favor of warm, mineral-rich plaster and light limestone. Accompanied by `#F5F4F0` for subtle container offsets.

Dark mode transitions invert the hierarchy into a nocturnal exhibition setting: deep obsidian canvas (`#121316`) framed by hairline concrete borders (`#2A2C32`), with warm ivory text (`#FBFBF9`) and radiant bronze accents (`#C5A059`).

## Typography

Typographic scale reflects the contrast between majestic architectural scales and minute drafting annotations.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Unapologetically editorial and graceful. Set with high vertical rhythm, deliberate line-height breathing room, and tight tracking on oversized scales to emphasize sculptural letterforms.
- **Body & Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, structural, and neutral. Its low-contrast geometry supports dense architectural specifications and lengthy descriptions without clashing with the serif headlines.
- **Drafting Annotations (`label-caps`):** Must always be set in full uppercase with wide tracking (`0.18em`). Used strictly for project coordinates, structural taxonomy, phase numbers, and section markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural grid of modernist steel-and-glass pavilions. Space is treated as an active material:

- **12-Column Asymmetric Grid:** Layouts run on a 12-column foundation where asymmetry is encouraged (e.g., a 4-column descriptive manifesto flanking an 8-column hero elevation render).
- **Drafting Grid Alignment:** Content edges strictly anchor to grid lines. Structural separators run continuous across the screen edge-to-edge as 1px hairlines.
- **Proportional Scaling Across Viewports:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 5rem margins, 3rem gutters. Dramatic vertical margins (`space-2xl`) between project portfolios.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 3rem margins, 2rem gutters. Elements condense proportionally; secondary metadata collapses under media.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Hero headlines fall back to dedicated mobile scale to prevent awkward hyphenation and orphans.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth is conveyed through architectural layering and material materiality rather than synthetic drop shadows:

- **Hairline Tectonic Frames:** Borders utilize 1px semi-transparent concrete hairlines (`rgba(142, 144, 150, 0.2)` on light surfaces; `rgba(229, 229, 226, 0.12)` on dark surfaces). Depth is established by interlocking these bounded planar zones.
- **Architectural Glassmorphism:** Translucent floating navigation docks and informational overlays utilize heavy backdrop blur (`24px` to `32px`) combined with high-tint surfaces (`rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.75)` in light mode and `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.8)` in dark mode). An ultra-thin inner border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) simulates bevel-cut architectural glass.
- **Ambient Monolith Occlusion:** For elevated sheets or modal dossiers, shadows are wide, diffuse, and tinted with the deep charcoal palette: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08)`. Harsh, dark, or generic black shadows are prohibited.

## Shapes

The design system enforces an uncompromising **Sharp (`0`)** geometric geometry:
- Every element—buttons, image containers, input fields, modals, and preview tiles—is cut with sharp `0px` corners, echoing planar masonry, raw stone slabs, and honed structural steel.
- No rounded pills or softened corners are permitted anywhere in the primary UI. The sole exception is the circular cursor follower or floating technical camera toggles, which must remain pure 1:1 circles.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button:** Monolithic block with solid Obsidian (`#121316`) background, Warm Ivory (`#FBFBF9`) text, 0px border radius. Padding: `1rem 2.25rem`. Typography: `label-caps`. Hover triggers a seamless transition to Architectural Bronze (`#C5A059`) with text maintaining absolute contrast.
- **Secondary (Technical Outline):** 1px hairline border (`#8E9096` at 40% opacity), transparent background, Obsidian text. On hover: fill transitions to `#F5F4F0` with border color shifting to `#121316`.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text in `label-caps` paired with an extended 1px drafting arrow (`→`) that extends horizontally by 8px on interaction.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Structural indicators set in `label-caps`. 
- Rendered with a 1px border (`#E5E5E2`), background of `#F5F4F0`, and text in `#121316`. No internal corner rounding. Padding: `0.375rem 0.875rem`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat horizontal drafting baseline: Input fields feature transparent backgrounds with a single bottom hairline border (`1px solid #8E9096`).
- Floating field labels using `label-caps` in `#8E9096`. On focus, the bottom border elevates to `#121316` (or `#C5A059` in dark mode) via a smooth horizontal sweep animation.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Strict square perimeters with 0px corner radius. Hairline border in `#8E9096`. Checked states fill with solid Obsidian (`#121316`) containing a hairline bronze crossmark or interior square.

### Architectural Portfolio Cards
- Full-bleed media container with an edge-to-edge 1px hairline outer frame.
- Image ratio fixed at architectural proportions (16:9 for landscapes, 4:5 for vertical elevations).
- Image scale zooms subtly (`1.03x`) within its clipped sharp bounding container on hover, while an overlay glass metadata bar reveals project coordinates, year, and material taxonomy.

### Dossier Drawers & Modals
- Slides strictly from right or bottom as full-height monolithic slabs. Framed by a leading hairline border with ambient diffusion (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.06)`). Inner margins utilize generous `space-xl` breathing room.
